Works with Claude and ChatGPT

Ask your shop anything.

Connect Claude or ChatGPT to Get Bookking and ask about your day in plain English. “Who’s on my schedule tomorrow?” “How much did I take in this week?” “Did Maria text me back?”

How to connect

You need a Get Bookking account (shop owner) and a Claude or ChatGPT account. It takes about a minute.

Claude

  1. In Claude (web, desktop, or phone), open Settings → Connectors and search for Get Bookking. Not listed yet? Tap Add custom connector, name it “Get Bookking”, and enter https://getbookking.com/mcp.
  2. Tap Connect. A Get Bookking page opens. Sign in with your Get Bookking email and password.
  3. Choose whether Claude can take actions, then tap Allow.
  4. Start a new chat, make sure Get Bookking is on in the tools menu, and ask a question.

ChatGPT

  1. In ChatGPT, open Apps and search for Get Bookking. Not listed yet? Turn on Developer mode in Settings, create an app, and enter https://getbookking.com/mcp as the server URL.
  2. Tap Connect, sign in with your Get Bookking email and password, and tap Allow.
  3. Start a new chat, pick Get Bookking, and ask a question.

ChatGPT’s and Claude’s menus change often. If yours look different, look for “Connectors” or “Apps” in Settings.

Things to ask

  • What does my schedule look like this week?
  • Do I have any new booking requests?
  • Pull up my notes on Jordan.
  • How much did I make today?
  • Any unread messages I need to answer?
  • What services and prices are on my website?
  • Confirm Sam’s request for Friday.
  • Send Jordan a $50 deposit link.
  • Text Maria that I’m running 10 minutes late.

What it can do

Read: your schedule, booking requests, clients and notes, payments, messages, and your website’s services.

Take actions: confirm a booking request, text a client a deposit or payment link, and text a client.

You always approve first. Before anything is sent or changed, the assistant shows you exactly what it will do, like “Text Maria (555-0142): ‘Running 10 min late!’”, and waits for your yes. Texts and links go out from your shop’s number and show up in Messages like any other.

It can’t cancel, refund, or charge a saved card. Want reading only? Leave Can take actions off when you tap Allow, or turn it off later in Settings → AI assistant.

Keeping it safe

  • You sign in on getbookking.com. Claude and ChatGPT never see your Get Bookking password.
  • See and turn off connected apps anytime in Settings → AI assistant. They stop working right away.
  • Only the shop owner can connect an AI assistant.
  • What you ask, and what the assistant reads, is handled by the AI provider you connect (Anthropic for Claude, OpenAI for ChatGPT) under their own privacy terms.
