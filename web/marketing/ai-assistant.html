Early access

Ask your shop anything.

Connect Claude or ChatGPT to Get Bookking and ask about your day in plain English. “Who’s on my schedule tomorrow?” “How much did I take in this week?” “Did Maria text me back?”

How to connect

  1. Create a connection link. In Get Bookking on the web, go to Settings → Connect an AI assistant. Give it a name and tap Create connection link. Copy the link. It’s shown only once.
  2. Add it to your assistant, using the steps below for Claude or ChatGPT.

Claude

  1. On claude.ai or the Claude desktop app, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name it “Get Bookking”, paste your link, and tap Add.
  3. Start a new chat, turn on Get Bookking in the tools menu, and ask a question.

Using Claude Code? Run claude mcp add --transport http getbookking YOUR_LINK.

ChatGPT

  1. On chatgpt.com (web, not the phone app), open Plugins, tap +, then Add custom MCP server.
  2. Name it “Get Bookking” and paste your link as the server URL. Set Authentication to No authentication. Your link already includes your key.
  3. Check I understand and want to continue and create it. ChatGPT shows a warning because custom servers aren’t reviewed by OpenAI. That’s expected.
  4. Start a new chat, type @, pick Get Bookking, and ask a question.

Custom servers in ChatGPT need a paid plan, and some accounts must turn on Developer mode first under Settings. ChatGPT’s menus change often. If yours look different, see OpenAI’s help article on connecting custom MCP servers.

Things to ask

  • What does my schedule look like this week?
  • Do I have any new booking requests?
  • Pull up my notes on Jordan.
  • How much did I make today?
  • Any unread messages I need to answer?
  • What services and prices are on my website?
  • Confirm Sam’s request for Friday.
  • Send Jordan a $50 deposit link.
  • Text Maria that I’m running 10 minutes late.

What it can do

Read: your schedule, booking requests, clients and notes, payments, messages, and your website’s services.

Take actions: confirm a booking request, text a client a deposit or payment link, and text a client.

You always approve first. Before anything is sent or changed, the assistant shows you exactly what it will do, like “Text Maria (555-0142): ‘Running 10 min late!’”, and waits for your yes. Texts and links go out from your shop’s number and show up in Messages like any other.

It can’t cancel, refund, or charge a saved card. Want reading only? Turn off Can take actions for that connection in Settings.

Keeping it safe

  • Your link works like a password. Don’t share it or post it anywhere.
  • Revoke a link anytime in Settings and it stops working right away.
  • Only the shop owner can create links.
  • What you ask, and what Claude reads, is handled by the AI provider you connect (Anthropic for Claude, OpenAI for ChatGPT) under their own privacy terms.
