Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026  ·  Get Bookking is operated by Software by King LLC ("we," "us," "our")

Software by King LLC (d/b/a Get Bookking) (“Get Bookking,” “Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) operates a business-management and booking software platform for independent businesses, including salons, studios, shops, and charter operators. Our Services include our website, hosted business websites and custom-domain functionality, iOS mobile application and web-based tools, appointment and trip booking, calendars and team scheduling, service and product listings, online and in-person payment functionality, optional product-shop and shipping tools, email, SMS, push and social-media messaging features, digital waivers, Google Business Profile and review tools, optional social-media advertising creation and management tools, customer-management tools, domain services, reporting, and related software, integrations, and services (collectively, the “Services”).

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, storage, disclosure, and other processing of Personal Data when you use the Services and the choices and rights you may have regarding that data (“Privacy Policy”). This Privacy Policy applies to information processed by Get Bookking through the Services, including information relating to subscribing businesses and their owners and invited team members (each a “Business Customer”), prospective Business Customers, and individuals who interact with a Business Customer through its hosted site or other enabled features, including persons who book services or trips, purchase products, make payments, or exchange messages (each an “End Customer”). Use of the Services is also subject to our Terms of Use and any applicable agreement with a Business Customer. This Privacy Policy does not apply to the independent practices of Business Customers or companies that we do not own or control.

We use Personal Data to provide, secure, support, administer, and improve the Services and for the other purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Unless otherwise defined in this Privacy Policy, capitalized terms used here have the same meanings as in our applicable Terms of Use.

Definitions

Cookies: Cookies are small pieces of data stored on or accessed through your device and may include similar technologies such as pixels, tags, local storage, and software development kits.

Data Controller / Business: Depending on the context and applicable law, Get Bookking may act as a data controller, business, or similar entity when it determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data, including for Business Customer accounts, subscriptions, product administration, security, billing, analytics, support, and product communications. A Business Customer may separately act as the controller, business, or similar responsible party for End Customer booking, order, payment, message, and other information that it chooses to collect or use through the Services.

Data Processors / Service Providers: A data processor, service provider, contractor, or subprocessor is a person or company that processes Personal Data for another party. Get Bookking uses third-party providers to support functions such as authentication, cloud hosting and storage, domain routing and registration, payment processing, shipping, email, SMS and push delivery, social-media messaging, analytics, maps, customer support, security, and mobile-app distribution.

Personal Data: Personal Data means information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with an individual or household, as defined by applicable law.

Service: Service means Get Bookking’s business-management and booking platform and related website, hosted business sites, custom-domain functionality, mobile application, web-based tools, software, integrations, and features for scheduling, bookings, products, payments, shipping, communications, domains, team management, reporting, and other Business Customer operations.

Usage Data: Usage Data is information collected automatically from the use of the Services or the Service infrastructure, such as log records, device and browser information, timestamps, feature activity, and performance data.

User: “User” means any individual who accesses or interacts with the Services, including a Business Customer owner or invited team member, prospective Business Customer, End Customer, website visitor, or other user of the Services.
