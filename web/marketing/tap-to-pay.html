Tap to Pay on iPhone.
Accept contactless payments right on your iPhone.

Take Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and digital wallets in Get Bookking — no extra card reader. Payments run through Stripe so you get paid securely.

Get started free Get early access

Available in the U.S. on compatible iPhone models · Stripe Connect required

How it works

Charge in a few taps

Enter the amount in Get Bookking, hold the customer’s contactless card or phone near the top of your iPhone, and payment is processed through Stripe — receipts and records stay with your business.

  • In the shop or on the go Take payment at the chair, the front desk, or wherever the job finishes.
  • Clear totals before you charge Customers see what they pay, including processing fees when you pass them through.
  • Same app for bookings and payments Connect Stripe once, then use Tap to Pay on iPhone alongside deposits, links, and manual checkout.
Getting started

Ready in three steps

  1. 1

    Get Get Bookking

    Sign up and open the iOS app on a compatible iPhone.

  2. 2

    Connect Stripe

    Finish Stripe Connect setup in Payment settings so you can accept charges and get paid out.

  3. 3

    Start accepting payments

    Accept Apple’s Tap to Pay on iPhone terms when prompted, then charge with Tap to Pay on iPhone.

No extra hardware

Your iPhone is the reader

Accept contactless cards and digital wallets with Tap to Pay on iPhone in Get Bookking. No separate card reader to buy, charge, or carry.

Privacy and security

Built on Apple and Stripe protections

Tap to Pay on iPhone uses the security and privacy features of iPhone. Payment card data is not stored on your device. Stripe handles card processing and Connect payouts for your business.

PIN entry on iPhone is supported for eligible transactions when required by the card network.
