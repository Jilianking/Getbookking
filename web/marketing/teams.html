Split pay

Everyone gets paid. The studio gets its share.

Each team member connects their own Stripe account, so client payments land with the person who did the work.

Set a split for each person: the percentage they keep and what goes to the studio.
Apply it to services, deposits, or both.
The studio share is sent after each payment, and reversed automatically on refunds.
Turn off payments for anyone who shouldn’t take them.
Website

A booking page for every person on your team.

Your studio site gets a team page, and each person you choose gets their own page clients can book from.

Each member gets a page and booking link at /team/their-name.
Choose who appears on the team page and on your home page.
Show a bio, job title, and portfolio photos for each person.
Let teammates edit their own bio and portfolio, or keep that to yourself.
